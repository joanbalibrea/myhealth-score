How this score works

Only answered scoring items contribute. Each domain is weighted by its available scoring coverage; unanswered domains are N/A. Age, sex, calories, sport, and support history do not change the score. Completeness includes contextual answers and applicable follow-up questions.

The current four mood choices map to 1, 6, 8, and 10 from least to most favorable. Combined Rarely / Never anxiety maps to 9; a few days to 6; most days to 3. Substance-frequency ranges use midpoint scores. Former smoking needs a quit duration; current smoking needs a cigarettes/day category to contribute a tobacco score.
